About the Software

Built for organisations that have to defend their numbers

Carbon Ledger is a cloud-based greenhouse gas accounting platform for companies that report emissions to regulators, investors, customers or the public. It covers the full inventory: Scope 1 direct emissions, Scope 2 purchased energy — dual-reported location-based and market-based — and all fifteen Scope 3 value-chain categories defined by the GHG Protocol.

Most carbon tools stop at a number. Carbon Ledger is built around the harder requirement: showing your work. Every record stores the activity data, the exact emission factor version applied, the gas-by-gas breakdown, the GWP set used, the data-quality tier, and a complete audit trail of who entered and who approved it. When an assurer, a regulator or your own board asks where a figure came from, the answer is one click away.

Who it's for

Sustainability & ESG teams

Own the inventory end to end without living in twenty spreadsheets that no two people can reconcile.

Facility & operations managers

Enter fuel, electricity and refrigerant data through guided flows that need no carbon-accounting expertise.

Finance & disclosure officers

Produce CSRD, CDP and GRI figures from one inventory, so filings to different bodies cannot contradict each other.

Regulated facilities in Abu Dhabi

Generate a filled EAD MRV workbook from data already in the system — no parallel process.

Auditors & verifiers

Trace any reported tonne to its source document, factor version and approver through an immutable audit log.

Suppliers in your value chain

Respond to Scope 3 data requests through a secure portal — no account, no password, one link.

The problem it solves

Corporate carbon inventories are still assembled by hand: fuel logs emailed as PDFs, utility bills retyped, factors copied from a decade-old spreadsheet, Scope 3 estimated once and never revisited. The result is an inventory nobody can reproduce. When the factor library updates, prior years silently shift. When a supplier restates, nothing downstream recalculates. When an assurer asks for evidence, someone spends three weeks reconstructing it.

Carbon Ledger replaces that with a single system of record where the calculation is explicit, factors are versioned and locked to each record, and evidence is attached at the point of entry.

Key Features

Every capability described here is working software, not roadmap

Scope 1

Guided Scope 1 entry

A three-step flow — Source, Data, Review — across stationary, mobile and fugitive sub-categories, covering natural gas, diesel, LPG, coal grades, biofuels, jet fuel, refrigerants, SF₆ and PFCs. CO₂e previews live as you type.

Scope 2

Purchased energy, dual-reported

Thirteen supply arrangements — grid electricity, green tariffs, PPAs and VPPAs, landlord-supplied, on-site third-party renewables, CHP, EV charging — each carrying both a location-based and market-based result.

Scope 3

All fifteen categories

Upstream categories 1–8 and downstream 9–15, each described in plain language at the point of entry, with spend-based input-output factors where activity data does not exist.

Classification

Scope Finder

Describe an activity in your own words and Scope Finder returns the right scope and category, with a deterministic keyword fallback so it never leaves you without an answer.

Document capture

Utility bill reading

Upload an electricity or fuel bill as PDF or image. Consumption, date, cost and supplier are extracted through a cascading OCR chain with AI-assisted structured reading, sanity-checked before reaching your inventory.

Value chain

Supplier surveys, no login

Send a one-time secure link. Suppliers answer, save partially and return later without an account. Tagged answers convert automatically into draft Scope 3 records against the right category.

Assurance

Review queue & validation

Records arrive as drafts. A filterable queue flags missing facility or department, shows the data source — import, manual or API — and supports bulk validate, reject or delete.

Assurance

Data quality scoring

Primary, secondary and estimated classification produces a rolling quality score with a twelve-month trend and per-supplier scorecard, so engagement effort goes where it moves the number.

Disclosure

Three frameworks, one inventory

CSRD/ESRS E1, CDP Climate Change and GRI 305 datapoints emit from a single computed inventory — so filings are internally consistent by construction, not by cross-checking.

Regulatory

EAD / EU-ETS MRV layer

Opt in per facility and per year. Source streams, emission sources, measuring instruments and tiered uncertainty feed a filled EAD submission workbook — while ordinary users never see a tier or an NCV.

Analysis

Hotspot & Pareto analysis

Rank emissions by source, facility, department or supplier with running cumulative percentages, so the few contributors driving most of your footprint surface immediately.

Traceability

Audit trail on every change

Emission records, factors, targets and all MRV entities write to an immutable audit log — who changed what, when, and from which value to which.

How teams experience it

A facility manager photographs an electricity bill and uploads it; consumption, date and cost are read out and staged as a draft. An analyst reviews the queue, confirms the facility, and validates — the factor version is already locked to the record. Meanwhile a procurement lead sends survey links to forty suppliers, who respond without ever creating an account, their answers landing as draft Scope 3 records against the right categories.

At quarter end, the analyst opens Analytics, sees that three sources account for most of the movement, and drills in. At year end, the disclosure officer generates ESRS E1, CDP and GRI packs from the same inventory — and, for the regulated facility, exports the EAD workbook. Nothing was re-keyed. Nothing has to be reconciled.

Technical Highlights

Architecture built around traceability and tenant isolation

Isolation

Tenant scoping, deny by default

Company isolation is enforced by a global query scope on every core model. A user without a resolved company context returns nothing — the fallback is refusal, never an unscoped query.

Access

Granular role-based permissions

Every action is permission-gated, with roles from Super Admin to standard User, a full roles and permissions interface, and per-user navigation restrictions.

Traceability

Immutable audit trail

Emission records, factors, targets and all MRV entities write every change to an audit log surfaced inside the application.

Integrity

Factor versioning & locking

Factors carry dataset version, validity window, issuing organisation and country. Records pin the version used, so historical inventories stay stable.

Correctness

Dimensional unit conversion

A dedicated conversion service normalises units and rejects incompatible ones, rather than trusting that whoever typed "gal" meant what you did.

Reconciliation

MRV decomposition that agrees

The EU-ETS decomposition is asserted to reconcile with the combined-factor result, so regulatory and management views of a facility cannot silently diverge.

Interface

Server-rendered and responsive

A fast, conventional web interface with server-side tables that stay responsive at inventory scale, usable on a phone in a plant room.

Assisted

AI where it earns its place

Bill extraction and scope classification use AI with deterministic fallbacks, sanity ceilings and recorded confidence. AI proposes, the audit trail records, a human approves.
